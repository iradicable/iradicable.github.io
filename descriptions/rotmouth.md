 # Rotmouth

A village of only a few dozen people built high upon a mountain, surrounding a small lake. 

The settlement is named after the lake, which in turn got its name from the water's peculiar taste. It is mostly known for the Rotmouth brew, a thick and dark ale made from local crop.

Rotmouth was briefly under the influence of an elemental being that infected the waters, causing any who drink the water to become its thrall.
