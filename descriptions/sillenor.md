# Sillenor

Capital of the Kingdom of Ubera sitting between coast and mountain, making it near impossible to siege. While prosperous and beautiful, it is built with clear pragmatism and lacks the very heights of opulence found in certain other major cities.

The royal citadel is built into the face of the mountain overlooking the rest of the city. Winding stairs and paths lead into the city proper which is built on a slope, guaranteeing a view of the ocean no matter where you find yourself.
